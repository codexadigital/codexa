Questions, answered

Before their first visit.

Everything pet parents usually ask before grooming or daycare.

How long does a full groom take?＋
Most full grooms take around 2–3 hours, depending on breed, coat condition and temperament. We never rush a nervous pet.
Do you groom puppies?＋
Yes. Puppy introduction sessions are designed to make the salon feel positive and familiar before longer grooming appointments.
Can I book grooming and daycare together?＋
Absolutely. Select both during booking and we’ll organise the day around your pet’s grooming slot and suitable rest time.
What should I bring for daycare?＋
Bring any required medication, feeding instructions and a familiar comfort item if helpful. We provide bowls, beds, enrichment and fresh water.
Are the prices final?＋
The website estimator is indicative. We confirm the final price after assessing coat condition, size and the service time required.
